## Structure of a CSR (ICH E3)
Source: ICH E3 guideline https://database.ich.org/sites/default/files/E3_Guideline.pdf ; E3 Q&As https://database.ich.org/sites/default/files/E3_Q%26As_R1_Q%26As.pdf
- Sections 1 to 8: title page, synopsis, contents, abbreviations, ethics, investigators and administrative structure, introduction, study objectives.
- 9 Investigational plan: design, population, treatments, assessments, data quality, statistical methods, sample size, changes in conduct or analyses.
- 10 Study patients: disposition and protocol deviations.
- 11 Efficacy evaluation: data sets analysed, demographics and baseline, compliance, efficacy results, conclusions.
- 12 Safety evaluation: exposure, adverse events, deaths and serious events, laboratory values, vital signs, safety conclusions. Narratives in 12.3.2 or 14.3.3.
- 13 Discussion and overall conclusions.
- 14 Tables, figures and graphs referred to but not included in the text: 14.1 demographics, 14.2 efficacy, 14.3 safety (14.3.2 listings of deaths and serious events).
- 15 Reference list. 16 Appendices: 16.1 study information (protocol, sample CRF, statistical documentation), 16.2 patient data listings, 16.3 CRFs, 16.4 individual patient listings.
- E3 allows structural adaptation; it is not a rigid template.

## Structured protocols and content reuse
- ICH M11 (CeSHarP): Step 4 on 19 Nov 2025; FDA final guidance announced 22 May 2026; EU effective 11 Jun 2026. Three parts: guideline, protocol template, technical specification (data elements). https://database.ich.org/sites/default/files/ICH_Step4_M11_Final_Guideline_2025_1119.pdf ; M11 explainer May 2026 https://database.ich.org/sites/default/files/ICH%20M11%20Explainer_May2026.pdf
- TransCelerate Digital Data Flow uses CDISC USDM for study-design reuse; the Clinical Content and Reuse template suite (protocol, SAP, CSR) last coordinated release 2024, CC&R completed March 2026. https://www.transceleratebiopharmainc.com/initiatives/clinical-content-reuse/
- Neither M11 nor TransCelerate templates on their own give a validated protocol-to-CSR pipeline (interpretation).

## Timelines today
- EMWA survey (2014, data 2008 to 2013), moderately complex Phase III: database lock to final CSR about 74 working days (about 15 weeks) summed by stage; final TLFs to first draft about 17 working days; first draft to final about 26. https://emwa.org/wp-content/uploads/Media/media/1965/2047480614z2e000000000211.pdf
- Lean authoring paper (8 companies, 2026): average 5.25 weeks from data availability to CSR approval at participating companies. https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s43441-026-00980-6 (same paper as PMC13526120)
- Pfizer PK-CSR.Gen (Clin Transl Sci, 15 Jun 2026): 7 reports, PK sections only, about 18.2 minutes per draft, about 90% relative quality by blinded raters; not full CSRs, and revision time was not measured. https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13269176/

## Vendors
- Certara CoAuthor: Word-based authoring with regulatory templates, structured content reuse and source-restricted generative AI; CSRs, protocols, narratives. https://www.certara.com/coauthor/
- Yseop Copilot: source-driven first drafts of whole documents in Word and Veeva Vault (CSRs, narratives, CSS/CES, IBs, PK reports, QOS). https://yseop.com/automate-medical-regulatory-authoringworkflows/
- Weave Bio Submission Builder: dossier authoring with sentence-level source tracing and AI reviewers for cross-document inconsistencies and numerical mismatches (Feb 2026 release); NDA workflow with Parexel (29 Apr 2026). https://www.weave.bio/news/ ; Takeda study of IND nonclinical summaries (Sept 2025).
- Pattern across vendors: templates plus source-grounded drafting plus human review; none publicly claims unattended generation of a complete submission.

## Animal health (FDA CVM)
- CVM GFI #104: content and format of effectiveness and target animal safety technical sections and final study reports (non-food animals). https://www.fda.gov/media/70050/download
- CVM GFI #85 / VICH GL9 (Good Clinical Practice), section 7 for final study report content. https://www.fda.gov/media/70333/download
- CVM GFI #185 / VICH GL43 (target animal safety), section 2.8 study reports; 21 CFR Part 58 for GLP safety studies. https://www.fda.gov/media/70438/download
- A technical section assembles cover letter, contents, integrated study summaries, individual study reports, other information, foreign experience, proposed labelling and FOI summary material. Each final study report documents 1 study, following the protocol's format.
